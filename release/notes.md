Duo Receiver turns your Apple Silicon Mac into a companion for Duo Trackpad on iPhone.

1. Open the DMG and drag **Duo Receiver** to **Applications**.
2. Open the app and allow Accessibility and Local Network access when prompted.
3. Open Duo Trackpad on your iPhone, select your Mac, and enter its pairing code.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later, the iPhone beta, and a shared local network. Pair once; later launches reconnect automatically.

This is an early beta. Some gestures use keyboard shortcuts and depend on the foreground app. Force Click is not supported. Use a trusted local network; the custom pairing protocol has not had an independent security audit.

The app and DMG are signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. SHA256SUMS is included to verify the download.
